## Overview

Matahari runs several online storefronts: Matahari.com, SUKO and MaxMurah. I work on the Go microservices backend (gRPC + REST) that centralizes their catalog, inventory, customer accounts and orders. I'm the top contributor to 4 of its 7 services.

## The problem

Each channel used to have its own integration with vendors. Every new channel or vendor change meant more duplicated work, and product data was spread across separate pipelines.

## What I built

- **Integration service, from scratch.** All vendor product, stock, image and fulfillment data now enters the platform through this one service. It replaces the separate per-channel integrations with one shared catalog.
- **Storefront product search** with filters and facets, built on Typesense.
- **A secure product image pipeline** on object storage (MinIO).
- **A bulk stock sync** designed to handle 60,000 SKUs every 5 minutes.
- **Customer accounts integrated with the group loyalty CRM:** OTP activation, password reset and loyalty profiles.
- **A test-first restructure of the user service**, which I led, with enforced module boundaries.
